Política de uso aceitável

Versão 1.0 · Data de vigência: 21 de agosto de 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) applies to every person who accesses or uses systems, portals, dashboards, maps, applications, tools, interfaces, or other technology made available by BORESPOT LLC (“BORESPOT”). This AUP forms part of and is incorporated into the BORESPOT User Terms of Use in accordance with the versioning and update procedures established in those User Terms.

1. Authorized Business Use Only

You may use BORESPOT systems only for legitimate business purposes related to the organization that authorized your access, within the permissions assigned to your account, and in accordance with the User Terms, this AUP, and the applicable User Security Policy. You may not use BORESPOT systems for an unauthorized personal, competitive, unlawful, or third-party purpose.

2. No Shared Accounts or Credentials

  • Do not share usernames, passwords, or authentication codes.
  • Do not provide another person with access through your account.
  • Do not use another user’s credentials.
  • Do not create or maintain shared-user credentials unless BORESPOT expressly authorizes them.

Each Authorized User must use their own authorized account.

3. No Unauthorized Third-Party Viewing or Access

You may not permit an unauthorized person to view, navigate, inspect, operate, observe, or analyze BORESPOT systems through your authenticated account, including by screen sharing, video call, remote-control session, use of your logged-in computer, recorded walkthrough, or displaying restricted BORESPOT systems to developers, consultants, investors, competitors, or other unauthorized persons.

4. No Screenshots, Recording, Copying, or Capture Without BORESPOT Authorization

You may not create screenshots, screen recordings, photographs, videos, recordings, copies, reproductions, exports, or similar captures of BORESPOT systems, BORESPOT Technology, or Confidential Information unless expressly authorized by BORESPOT in writing, through an electronic communication, or through a BORESPOT feature that expressly permits the action.

Any authorization is limited to the specific purpose for which it was granted. Authorization does not permit redistribution, publication, disclosure to additional unauthorized persons, competitive analysis, reverse engineering, reproduction, system development, removal of proprietary or confidentiality notices, or retention beyond the authorized purpose unless BORESPOT expressly permits continued retention.

Upon completion of the authorized purpose, the material must be securely deleted or otherwise reasonably destroyed unless BORESPOT expressly authorizes continued retention.

5. No Scraping or Unauthorized Automation

You may not use robots, crawlers, scraping tools, scripts, browser automation, or similar technology to access or extract information from BORESPOT systems unless BORESPOT has expressly authorized that use. You may not circumvent rate limits, technical restrictions, or other controls.

6. No Reverse Engineering or Unauthorized Technical Analysis

You may not use BORESPOT systems for unauthorized technical analysis intended to discover or reproduce non-public architecture, source code, algorithms, workflows, automation logic, processes, data structures, models, or other proprietary functionality.

7. No Competitive Misuse

You may not use BORESPOT systems, BORESPOT Confidential Information, trade secrets, or protected BORESPOT Technology to recreate BORESPOT functionality; develop or help develop a competing or substitute system; provide protected system information to a developer, investor, consultant, or competitor; benchmark the system for unauthorized competitive development; train or instruct another person how to reproduce protected BORESPOT functionality using protected information; or knowingly assist or coordinate development based on protected BORESPOT information.

8. No Assistance to Unauthorized Third Parties

You may not knowingly assist another person in obtaining or using BORESPOT information in a manner prohibited by the User Terms or this AUP, including by providing screenshots, recordings, descriptions, workflow information, technical observations, credentials, authenticated access, system demonstrations, or other protected information for unauthorized analysis, reproduction, reverse engineering, competitive development, or other prohibited use.

9. No Security Circumvention

  • Do not bypass authentication, permissions, or access controls.
  • Do not attempt to elevate privileges or probe restricted portions of the system.
  • Do not interfere with logging or monitoring.
  • Do not disable or circumvent security features.
  • Do not exploit or attempt to exploit a vulnerability.

10. No Malicious Activity

  • Do not introduce malware or malicious code.
  • Do not attempt denial-of-service activity or interfere with system availability.
  • Do not corrupt or destroy data.
  • Do not compromise another account.
  • Do not use BORESPOT systems to attack any person, system, or network.

11. No Unauthorized Data Access

You may access only information made available to you for authorized Customer purposes. You may not attempt to obtain another Customer’s data, another user’s private information, restricted administrative information, or internal BORESPOT information that your account is not authorized to access. If information appears to have been displayed to you by mistake, stop accessing it and notify BORESPOT.

12. Protection of Confidential and Proprietary Information

Information identified as Confidential, Proprietary, BORESPOT Confidential & Proprietary, Authorized Users Only, or similar terminology must be handled as restricted information. The absence of a visual marking does not automatically mean non-public BORESPOT information is unrestricted. You may not remove, crop, hide, obscure, or alter confidentiality or proprietary notices.

13. Lawful Use

You may not use BORESPOT systems in violation of applicable law, to facilitate fraud, to impersonate another person, to interfere with lawful rights of others, or for any purpose prohibited by the User Terms.

14. 811 Information, Ticket Expiration, and Safe Use

BORESPOT systems may present administrative information relating to 811 tickets and third-party utility responses. You may not represent or treat a BORESPOT system status as a BORESPOT physical inspection, utility locate, certification that markings are physically present, determination that a location is safe to excavate, or authorization from BORESPOT to begin excavation.

Multiple tickets and expiration dates may be displayed for the same or overlapping work area. Do not rely on map color or administrative status alone. Before field activity, review the applicable ticket number or numbers and corresponding expiration information presented by BORESPOT. A newer ticket does not automatically make an earlier ticket invalid, and a Clear or similar administrative status does not extend a ticket beyond its applicable expiration.

The Customer and its field personnel remain responsible for physical field verification, field conditions, applicable safe-digging procedures, independently applicable legal duties, and the operational decision whether excavation may legally and safely begin or continue. Nothing in this Section requires users to duplicate BORESPOT’s contracted administrative 811-support work.

15. Reporting Problems

You must promptly report suspected unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, credential compromise, suspicious account activity, unexpected access to restricted information, security vulnerabilities, or other material misuse of BORESPOT systems. Do not publicly disclose a suspected BORESPOT security vulnerability before giving BORESPOT a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

16. Enforcement

BORESPOT may restrict, suspend, or terminate access for conduct that violates the version of this AUP validly applicable to the user. These enforcement grounds do not limit BORESPOT’s broader rights under the User Terms to suspend, restrict, or terminate access, with or without prior notice, when BORESPOT determines such action is necessary or appropriate. BORESPOT may also preserve logs or evidence, notify the Customer, and pursue remedies available under the User Terms or applicable law.

17. Updates and Versioning

BORESPOT may update this AUP independently from the User Terms and User Security Policy. Each version will have its own version number and effective date. An update to this AUP does not, by itself, change the version number of the User Terms.

Non-material updates may become applicable through reasonable electronic notice in accordance with the User Terms. If BORESPOT determines that an update materially increases, changes, or adds substantive user obligations or restrictions, BORESPOT will require affirmative electronic acceptance before making that materially updated version applicable to the user for continued access. BORESPOT may maintain records identifying which version applies to each user.
