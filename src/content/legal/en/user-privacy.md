This Customer & Authorized User Privacy Notice ("Notice") explains how BORESPOT LLC ("BORESPOT," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, protects, and retains personal information in connection with Customer relationships and access to BORESPOT systems. This Notice is provided for transparency and does not replace the BORESPOT User Terms of Use, Acceptable Use Policy, User Security Policy, or the separate agreement between BORESPOT and a Customer.

## 1. Scope

This Notice applies to personal information BORESPOT processes in connection with Customer relationships, Authorized User access, BORESPOT portals and systems, account administration, support, security, contractual acceptance records, and related service operations. This Notice does not govern public website or referral activities, which may be subject to a separate privacy notice.

## 2. Information We May Collect

- Identity, business-role, and contact information, such as name, email address, company, job title or role, and business contact details.
- Account and access identifiers, such as user ID, account ID, authentication-related records, and access status.
- Acceptance and contractual records, including versions of User Terms, policies and notices presented, effective dates, acceptance-form version, date and time, IP address, submission ID, and related audit records.
- Technical and security information, such as login records, authentication events, IP address, device or browser information when available, security events, and system activity logs.
- Service and support information, including communications, requests, and information reasonably necessary to administer Customer services and user access.

## 3. How We Obtain Information

We may receive information directly from you; from the Customer that authorizes your access or Services; automatically through BORESPOT systems and security logs; from service providers that support the purposes described in this Notice; and, when reasonably necessary to perform Customer-authorized services, from applicable 811 centers, utility operators, locators, portals, and related systems or communications.

## 4. How We Use Information

- Create, verify, administer, support, restrict, or terminate Authorized User access.
- Provide and support contracted services and related Customer operations.
- Authenticate users and protect accounts, systems, Customers, and BORESPOT Technology.
- Maintain evidence of contractual acceptance, policy versions, authorization, and other legal or audit records.
- Detect, investigate, prevent, or respond to fraud, misuse, security incidents, unauthorized access, copying, disclosure, or other violations.
- Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, enforce, or defend legal rights and claims.
- Troubleshoot, maintain, improve, and develop BORESPOT services, systems, security, and user experience.
- Perform internal analytics, operational planning, forecasting, business intelligence, service improvement, product development, and strategic decision-making. Where reasonably practicable, BORESPOT may use aggregated or de-identified information for broader analysis.

## 5. No Sale or Independent Third-Party Marketing

BORESPOT does not sell Authorized User personal information. BORESPOT does not disclose Customer- or Authorized User-specific personal information to competitors or other third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 6. How We May Disclose Information

BORESPOT may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Notice, including to:

- Service providers, contractors, and professional advisers supporting hosting, forms, electronic acceptance, authentication, security, communications, support, storage, internal systems, analytics, legal, accounting, tax, audit, or compliance functions.
- Applicable 811 centers, utility operators, locators, portals, and related systems or communications when reasonably necessary to perform Customer-authorized services or related administrative support.
- Government authorities, courts, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, or other persons when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or evidence.
- Parties involved in investigating or addressing suspected misuse, unauthorized access, fraud, security incidents, intellectual-property violations, or other misconduct.
- An affiliate, successor, purchaser, financing source, adviser, or other participant in a merger, reorganization, financing, sale, transfer, or similar corporate transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality or legal protections where applicable.

Nothing in this Section authorizes BORESPOT to disclose Customer Confidential Information to another customer or competitor except as permitted by the applicable Customer agreement or applicable law.

## 7. International Processing and Access

BORESPOT is a Wyoming limited liability company and may use service providers or infrastructure located in the United States or other countries. Personal information may be hosted, stored, accessed, or processed in the United States and may be accessed or processed by authorized BORESPOT personnel located outside the United States, including in Brazil or Paraguay, and by service providers located in other jurisdictions, as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Notice. BORESPOT uses reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and processing.

## 8. Retention

BORESPOT may retain personal information and related records for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, account administration, security, fraud prevention, compliance, auditing, contractual evidence, legal claims, applicable limitation periods, litigation holds, backup processes, tax, accounting, regulatory, insurance, enforcement, and defense needs. Acceptance records, access records, security records, and evidence relating to contractual, intellectual-property, or system-use obligations may be retained after an Authorized User's access ends where reasonably necessary for these purposes.

BORESPOT may delete, archive, de-identify, or aggregate information in accordance with its reasonable retention practices and applicable law. Backup copies may persist for a limited period through ordinary backup and disaster-recovery processes.

## 9. Security

BORESPOT uses commercially reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards appropriate to its size, the nature of the services, and the sensitivity of the information processed. No security method can guarantee absolute security, and this Notice does not promise that a security incident will never occur.

## 10. Privacy Requests and Rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other treatment of personal information. BORESPOT may take reasonable steps to verify identity and authority before responding and may retain or decline to delete information where permitted or required for contractual evidence, security, fraud prevention, compliance, legal claims, litigation holds, or other lawful purposes.
