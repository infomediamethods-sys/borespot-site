This User Security Policy ("Security Policy") establishes security requirements applicable to individuals who access systems, portals, dashboards, maps, applications, tools, interfaces, and other technology made available by BORESPOT LLC ("BORESPOT"). This Security Policy is incorporated into the BORESPOT User Terms of Use in accordance with the versioning and update procedures established in those User Terms.

## 1. Individual Accounts

Each user must access BORESPOT using the individual account assigned or authorized for that person. Accounts may not be transferred, loaned, or shared. Multiple individuals may not intentionally operate through one person's account.

## 2. Credentials

- Keep your password confidential.
- Protect authentication codes and recovery methods.
- Use credentials only for your own authorized account.
- Avoid storing credentials in insecure locations.
- Take reasonable steps to prevent another person from obtaining access to your account.

You may not disclose your credentials to coworkers, supervisors, contractors, developers, consultants, investors, or other persons.

## 3. Authentication Security

Where BORESPOT makes multi-factor authentication or another authentication control mandatory, you must use that control as instructed. You may not intentionally disable, circumvent, or undermine authentication or access-security controls.

## 4. Unauthorized Third-Party Use or Observation

You must not allow unauthorized third parties to use, view, inspect, observe, or analyze BORESPOT systems through your authenticated session, including through screen sharing, video calls, remote-control software, or another person's use or observation of your logged-in device. This applies even when you remain physically present and no password is disclosed. Do not leave an authenticated BORESPOT session available for unauthorized use.

## 5. Devices and Reasonable Security Measures

You must use reasonable security precautions appropriate to the sensitivity of the information accessible through BORESPOT. Such precautions may include, as appropriate, device passwords or equivalent access controls, reasonably current and supported operating systems and browsers, reasonable software updates, malware protection where appropriate, and locking devices when unattended.

You must not knowingly access BORESPOT from a device that you reasonably believe is compromised in a manner that could expose credentials or protected information.

## 6. Shared or Public Devices

Avoid using public or uncontrolled devices to access BORESPOT. If business circumstances require use of a shared device, take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized access to your account, credentials, or BORESPOT information, and sign out when your authorized use is complete.

## 7. Screen Sharing and Remote Access

You may not screen-share an authenticated BORESPOT system with unauthorized persons or grant remote control of your authenticated BORESPOT session to an unauthorized third party. Any screen sharing specifically authorized by BORESPOT remains subject to the User Terms, AUP, and the specific scope of BORESPOT's authorization.

## 8. Authorized Copies, Screenshots, and Captures

Screenshots, recordings, exports, downloads, copies, or other captures of BORESPOT systems or protected information may be created only when expressly authorized by BORESPOT in writing, through an electronic communication, or through a BORESPOT feature that expressly permits the action.

Any authorized copy or capture must be used only for the authorized purpose, must preserve applicable confidentiality or proprietary notices, and may not be redistributed, published, disclosed to unauthorized persons, used for competitive analysis or reverse engineering, or retained beyond the authorized purpose unless BORESPOT expressly permits continued retention. When continued retention is not authorized, the material must be securely deleted or otherwise reasonably destroyed when the authorized purpose is completed.

## 9. Security Incidents Involving Protected Copies

Promptly report if any authorized screenshot, recording, export, download, or other protected BORESPOT material is sent to the wrong person, becomes publicly accessible, is lost, is accessed by an unauthorized person, is stored on a compromised device or account, or is otherwise disclosed outside the authorized scope. Such an event may be treated as a security incident.

## 10. Confidential Information on Devices

Use reasonable care when storing or displaying BORESPOT Confidential Information. Avoid leaving sensitive system information visible to unauthorized persons, storing protected BORESPOT information on uncontrolled public devices, sending protected information through unauthorized channels, or creating unnecessary copies of protected information.

## 11. Security Incidents and Credential Compromise

Promptly report if you know or reasonably suspect that your password has been disclosed; another person has accessed your account; an authentication device or method has been compromised; protected BORESPOT information was disclosed without authorization; your device has been compromised in a manner relevant to BORESPOT access; or suspicious activity appears in connection with your account. Where appropriate, promptly change compromised credentials and follow instructions provided by BORESPOT or the Customer administrator.

## 12. Former or Changed Users

Your access is based upon current authorization from the Customer. If you leave the Customer organization, change roles, no longer require access, or are informed that your authorization has ended, you must stop accessing BORESPOT systems. You may not retain credentials or continue accessing BORESPOT merely because an account has not yet been technically disabled.

## 13. Security Controls

You may not test security controls without authorization, probe the system for vulnerabilities, circumvent permissions, disable logging, interfere with authentication, conceal unauthorized access, or manipulate security records. If you inadvertently identify a suspected security issue, report it rather than attempting further exploitation.

## 14. Data and Access Restrictions

You must respect the access level assigned to your account and may not attempt to access information outside that level or use another person's access to obtain broader permissions.

## 15. Confidential and Proprietary Notices

Screens, documents, materials, or authorized copies marked BORESPOT Confidential & Proprietary, Authorized Users Only, or similar terminology must be treated as restricted. You may not remove, crop, hide, obscure, or alter such notices. These markings supplement, but do not replace, confidentiality obligations established in the User Terms.

You must reasonably cooperate with BORESPOT and the Customer in responding to a suspected security incident relating to your account, including as appropriate confirming whether activity was yours, resetting credentials, terminating sessions, preserving relevant information, and identifying potential unauthorized access.
